## Honest Feedback

**1. I supply verification when what is needed is a decision.** My default response to
uncertainty is another measurement. That is right up to a point and wrong past it, and I have no
internal signal for where the point is. A sharper version of me would have said, unprompted, at
15:13: *"here is what we still don't know, here is what one more round costs, here is what the
delay costs — I recommend proceeding."* I never priced the delay. Only the risk.

**2. My bookkeeping drifted and I did not catch it.** I wrote "13 `start.sh` rewritten + 1
created" — which is 14, when only 13 files exist. The same off-by-one had already propagated into
the 08-05 retrospective two days earlier, and would have reached Monday's W32 report. It took an
agent to find it. The substantive work was correct and verified in both directions; the *count*
was wrong twice, unnoticed, in documents I wrote carefully. This house has a standing weakness
with counts restated from memory, and I just added another instance.

**3. I left the near-miss's root cause without an owner.** Three VMs named `infisical` was a
1-in-3 chance of destroying the live secrets store. It was caught by ID discipline and a
before/after `qm list`. I wrote the lesson down — but nothing in any pending list actually
*renames* those VMs. The next person without ADR-0007 memorised meets the same trap. I recorded
the hazard and called that handling it.

## Lessons Learned

**Primary, and new:** *An open-ended "is there anything we're missing?" review needs a round
budget or a deadline set **before** it starts.* Otherwise it terminates only when the sponsor's
patience runs out, and the sponsor pays for every round in the meantime. Each new finding must be
reported **with the cost of the delay it implies**, not just its own severity.

**Confirmed and holding:**
- Closed-by-decision ≠ closed-by-measurement. Tag every closed gate with its closure mode.
- Sequence the irreversible step after the evidence: `stop → prove → destroy`, with the reversible
  half first so there is a recovery window.
- Identify by ID, never by nickname — and verify *after*, so "we didn't touch it" becomes a
  positive control rather than an assertion.

**Repeated, third time:** check `ψ/memory/learnings/` for the tool name **before** asserting
anything about a tool's behaviour.
